Proposal Email Scorer

Paste a sales proposal email and get a 0–100 score before you send it. Checks subject length, spam trigger words, reading grade, length, call to action, personalisation and the you-to-us ratio. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded, no signup.

It grades an email you already wrote. If you still need the draft, the leaderr.io proposal generator writes a personalised proposal email from your site and your prospect’s site, and the leaderr.io dossier generator builds the company research to personalise it with.
